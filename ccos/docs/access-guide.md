# Who can see what: a guide for the owner

Open the portal and click **People and access**. Only owners see this page.

## Add a person
1. Click into **Add a person**. Type their name and email.
2. Pick a **Role**. The line under the role says what it lets them see.
3. Choose **Applies to**. For property-based roles, tick the properties they manage.
4. Click **Add person**. The portal shows a temporary password **once**. Send it to them privately (a call or secure message). It is not saved anywhere, so copy it before you leave the page.
5. They sign in with their email and the temporary password. The portal makes them choose their own password before showing anything.

## Common setups
| Who | Role | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Outside property manager (3PM) | Outside property manager | Tick only their own park |
| Ops staff who work two parks | Operations staff | Tick those two parks |
| Loren | Operations manager | A department: operations (all parks) |
| Acquisitions VA | Acquisitions coordinator | A department: acquisitions |
| Investor relations coordinator | Investor relations coordinator | A department: ir |

A person with more than one park holds one access line per park. You can add or remove one line at a time.

## Change access later
- **Give more access:** open the row's "Give more access" and tick more properties.
- **Remove access:** click Remove next to one line. Their next click in the portal uses the new access.
- **Switch someone off:** click Switch off. They cannot sign in, and a session already open stops on the next click. Switch on brings them back.
- **Forgot password:** click Reset password. You get a new temporary password, and they must choose their own again.

## Rules the portal enforces
- You cannot change your own access or switch yourself off.
- There is always at least one active owner.
- Only an owner can make another owner.
- A role can only be given where it makes sense, for example a coordinator for a department and an outside property manager for a property.
- Every change appears under **Recent changes**.

## What a person sees
Their screen shows only what their access allows. A property they were not given does not appear anywhere: not in lists, search, tasks or downloads. Typing the address of a page they cannot use shows "Forbidden".
