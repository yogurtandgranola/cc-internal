# Requirements from the 2026-11-10 meeting (Leo and Loren)

Source: Fieldy recording "MHP Portfolio Operations and Strategy".

## Vision
- One "MHP operating system" for the company. Not just a quick dashboard.
- One login per person. Each person sees their own dashboards: Loren sees ops, Dominic sees deals, IR sees investor views. Access is configurable per person.
- It is a wrapper that compiles sources: Notion, Asana, Google Drive, and MCP connections (Meow, Rent Manager).
- Access control: not everyone has a Cornell email, and each extra seat on the current plan costs about $20 a month, so access is by an allow-list the admin controls, not by email domain.

## Padcount requirements
1. Click into a property and see a renewals tile: insurance, LLC and entity docs, lease renewals.
2. Each renewal links to a Notion SOP ("how to do an insurance renewal") so a VA can run it.
3. Email reminders to the ops team 60 and 90 days before a renewal (insurance renewal triggers a rebid).
4. Link out to Asana tasks per property.
5. Show the business plan next to actuals: pro forma versus actual, plus the physical milestones and target by year (examples named: Lincoln Park, Emporia).
6. Property manager scorecard: collections, occupancy, tenant relations. Collections pass mark is 95%, and 90% means there is a problem. Show targets against pulled data.
